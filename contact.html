Reporting a guide problem

Please include the page URL, the sentence or step that appears incorrect, and—when relevant—the current official documentation that conflicts with it. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private customer data, or confidential documents.

Reporting a tool problem

Include the tool name, browser and operating system, the action you attempted, and what you expected to happen. For SeqName, use fabricated filenames if a real filename would reveal confidential information. For HotkeyRadar, describe the shortcut and environment without sending unrelated local data.

Corrections

Reports are reviewed against the current page, the tool behavior, and primary documentation when the issue depends on a third-party product. A report may lead to a correction, a clearer limitation, or no change if the claim cannot be verified.
